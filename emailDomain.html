Email Security & Domain Protection

Home

I created a custom business email domain, secured inbound email traffic, and enforced authentication controls to prevent spoofing or impersonation.

🛡️ Part 1: Securing inbound Email Traffic
- Verified domain ownership and configured Sophos Email Security as the inbound mail gateway
- Updated MX records to route emails through Sophos filtering
- Tested inbound email delivery and security inspection
- Integrated logs into Splunk to monitor emails with links that only become malicious when clicked

🌐 Part 2: Protecting the Domain’s Reputation
- SPF ✅  > Defined which servers are authorized to send mail for the domain
- DKIM 🔑  > Added a digital signature to verify message integrity and authenticity
- DMARC 🚨> Aligned SPF and DKIM results under one enforcement policy, ensuring that unauthenticated emails are rejected

Key skills applied & strengthened:

- Creating custom email domains
- Configuring SPF, DKIM, DMARC, and other MX records on the DNS management console/hosting provider panel
- Setting up inbound mail filtering and threat protection using a Secure Email Gateway (SEG)
- Setting up phishing campaigns and tracking user behaviour

This project added an enterprise-grade email defense layer to the network - improving visibility, detection, and overall resilience against email-borne threats.
